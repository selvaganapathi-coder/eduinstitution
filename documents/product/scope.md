# Product Scope

## In Scope for the Product

The planned system may include:

- Institution management
- Academic years
- Departments
- Programs and courses
- Semesters and subjects
- Curriculum
- Students and enrollment
- Faculty and staff
- Authentication
- Roles and permissions
- Attendance
- Timetable
- Examinations and internal assessment
- Results
- Assignments
- Fees and payments
- Notifications and announcements
- Documents
- Reports
- Certificates
- Student portal
- Faculty portal
- Administration portal
- Audit logs
- Settings and integrations

## Development Scope Rule

These modules are planned scope, not implemented functionality. They must be delivered incrementally as independent vertical slices.

No future module should be represented as complete until its implementation, testing, security review, performance review, UX review, and documentation satisfy the project Definition of Done.

## Current Scope

TASK-000 covers only architecture and repository foundation. Business workflows are explicitly out of scope.
